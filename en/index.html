Your day

Guidance, not a wall of numbers.

Your values are calculated on your iPhone from the data you share in Apple Health and compared with your own baseline. Estimates for guidance, not a diagnosis. How we calculate is explained in the Methodology.

  • StrainFrom 0 to 21, with a target zone for today.
  • RecoveryFrom HRV, resting heart rate and sleep, measured against your own baseline.
  • SleepA score from duration, stages, continuity, efficiency and timing.

Coach

One tip for your day.

  • One Coach tip a day that fits your night and your strain, calculated on your device.
  • Body energy and stress across the whole day.
  • Goals for steps, exercise and sleep.
  • No chat, no language model: every sentence from the Coach is based on your values.

Sleep and stress

Every stage of the night. Your stress through the day.

  • Sleep score from duration, stages, continuity, efficiency and timing, with trends over 7, 14 and 30 days.
  • Sleep stages come from Apple Health, for example from your Apple Watch. HealthUp+ has no sleep model of its own.
  • Stress from your heart rate against your own baseline, measured across the day. An estimate, not a measurement.
  • Breathing exercise in a 4-4-4-4 rhythm, whenever you want to wind down for a moment.

Body

Keep an eye on your fitness.

  • Fitness age as an estimate with sources, not a diagnosis and not a lab value.
  • Body composition with a 3D figure.
  • VO₂max, fitness trend and workouts with heart rate zones and vitals over time.

The calculations and their sources are in the Methodology.

Nutrition

Keep an eye on what you eat.

  • Food diary with barcode scan, search and a food database. Sources
  • Plan meals up to 7 days ahead, keep track of water, and build a streak for every day you log.
  • Backed up in your private iCloud, so your diary survives a reinstall. Only you have access, and you can turn it off in your profile.
  • Optional: AI photo analysis with your own API key, only after you agree. How it works

Nutritional values come from open databases and are mostly estimated for dishes. For products with a barcode, the information on the packaging applies. Recipe images and recipe texts are created with AI and checked by us; the images show serving suggestions.

League

Seven days with friends.

  • Round with friends: up to 100 points a day from steps, exercise and check-in. You decide at the start whether sleep counts.
  • Arena week: open to everyone, check-in and steps only, up to 50 points a day, no Apple Watch needed.
  • Leaderboard, rematch and more than 70 awards to collect. Check-in streaks earn awards.
  • Fair: others see your name, photo, points and fair-play notes, never your health data. In the Arena, your points give a rough idea of your steps.

What can be read publicly in the League is explained in the Privacy Policy, and the rules are in League and Community Rules.

iPhone Duo

Unfolded: more room for your day.

On iPhone Duo, HealthUp+ shows rings, Coach, body energy, stress and body metrics side by side. Sleep and stress open next to your day, and when half unfolded, the breathing exercise sits above the fold with the controls below.

Apple Watch

On your wrist, too.

  • Rings, Coach tip, League rank and check-in right on your watch.
  • Workouts with heart rate zones and the breathing exercise on your wrist.
  • Complications for your watch face.
  • With your permission, the watch saves workouts and mindful minutes to Apple Health.

The Apple Watch app requires watchOS 26.

Privacy

Your health is not for sale.

Calculated on your device

No servers of our own. Scores, Coach and models run on your iPhone and your Apple Watch.

No tracking, no ads

No third-party analytics, no selling of data.

Openly explained

Which services the app uses (Apple iCloud, GitHub, Open Food Facts and, optionally, your AI provider) is set out in the Privacy Policy.

What HealthUp+ is for. HealthUp+ helps healthy adults aged 16 and over put exercise, recovery, sleep and nutrition into context in everyday life. The app shows estimates based on your data in Apple Health and your own entries. HealthUp+ is not a medical device. The app does not detect, monitor, treat or prevent any disease and does not replace medical advice. If you feel ill or have symptoms, see a doctor. In an emergency, call the local emergency number (112 in the EU).
